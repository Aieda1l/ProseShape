# Weeknight Lemon Garlic Chickpea Pasta

In today's fast-paced world, finding time to cook a nourishing meal can feel like an uphill battle. That's where this lemon garlic chickpea pasta comes in — a vibrant, satisfying dish that comes together in just 25 minutes and uses pantry staples you probably already have on hand. Whether you're a busy parent, a hungry student, or simply someone who loves good food, this recipe is sure to become a staple in your rotation.

The secret lies in toasting the chickpeas until they're golden and slightly crisp, which adds a delightful texture that elevates the entire dish. A generous squeeze of lemon brightens everything up, while a handful of parmesan brings it all together.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (225 g) short pasta, such as rigatoni
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and patted dry
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan, plus more for serving

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta water, then drain.
2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chickpeas and cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden.
3. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes and cook for 1 minute, until fragrant.
4. Toss in the pasta, lemon zest and juice, parmesan, and about 1/2 cup of the reserved pasta water. Stir until glossy, adding more water as needed.

Enjoy this delicious, wholesome meal that's sure to impress!
