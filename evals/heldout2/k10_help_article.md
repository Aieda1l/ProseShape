# How do I export my notes?

Exporting your notes is a seamless and straightforward process that empowers you to take your ideas anywhere. Whether you're backing up your work or moving to a new device, Notewell makes it easy.

## Export a single note

1. Open the note you want to export.
2. Click the **More** menu (three dots) in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Export**, then choose **Markdown** or **PDF**.

You can also press `Ctrl+Shift+E` (or `Cmd+Shift+E` on Mac) to open the export menu directly.

## Export all your notes

1. Go to **Settings** > **Account** > **Data**.
2. Click **Export all notes**.
3. We'll email you a download link within 24 hours. The link expires after 7 days.

**Note:** Attachments larger than 50 MB are not included in bulk exports. To keep them, download them individually first.

We hope this helps! If you have any other questions, don't hesitate to reach out to our friendly support team. Happy writing!
