Bo the Little Tugboat

In a busy harbor full of great big ships, there lived a little tugboat named Bo.

Every morning, Bo chugged out to meet the ships. Toot, toot, toot went Bo, and he pulled them safely to the dock.

One afternoon, a thick gray fog rolled in. The big ships could not see the harbor. They could not see the dock. They could not even see each other.

"Oh no," said Bo. "The ships will bump into the rocks!"

Bo thought very hard. Then he remembered what Old Captain Mae always told him: "You don't need to be big to be brave."

So Bo did not hide. He chugged out into the fog as fast as his little engine could go. Toot... toot... toot went Bo.

The first ship heard him. Then the second ship heard him. Then the third. One by one, all three ships followed Bo's toots safely into the harbor.

When the fog lifted, the sun came out, warm and bright. The sailors cheered for Bo, and Bo felt proud all the way down to his propeller.

From that day on, Bo knew that being little didn't mean being unimportant. Everyone, no matter how small, has something special to offer the world.

The End.
