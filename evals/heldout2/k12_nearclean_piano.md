I sold my piano last week.

I'd been telling myself for almost ten years that I would start playing again. It sat in the corner of the living room, mostly holding up mail and a lamp. Every time I moved, I paid two men to carry it up or down a flight of stairs, and every time I quietly promised it that this would be the apartment where I'd finally practice.

The woman who bought it came with her daughter, who is maybe seven. The girl sat down before anyone said she could and played the first few bars of something I almost recognized. Her mother apologized. I told her not to.

I thought I'd feel worse. Mostly I feel lighter, and a little embarrassed that it took me this long. Letting go of the piano didn't mean letting go of music. It meant admitting I'd stopped.
