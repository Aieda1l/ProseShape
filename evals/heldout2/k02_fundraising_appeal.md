Subject: Help Us Reach 500 Families Before Winter

Dear Friend,

As the days grow shorter and the temperatures begin to drop, we are reminded of the profound importance of warmth, safety, and community. At Riverbend Community Pantry, we believe that no family should have to choose between heating their home and putting food on the table.

This past year, thanks to the unwavering generosity of supporters like you, we distributed more than 84,000 meals and provided emergency heating assistance to 312 households across Clay County. But the need is growing. Requests for help have risen by 27% since last fall, and our shelves are emptier than they have been in years.

Take Maria, a mother of three who lost her job at the cannery in August. When she came to us, she wasn't just looking for groceries — she was looking for hope. Today, her family has a full pantry, a warm apartment, and a path forward.

Our goal is to reach 500 families before December 21. A gift of $50 provides a week of groceries for a family of four, and a gift of $150 covers a month of heating assistance.

Together, we can make a real difference. Please give today at riverbendpantry.org/winter.

With heartfelt gratitude,

Elena Ruiz
Executive Director, Riverbend Community Pantry
