# Introducing Tidewell 2.4: Smarter Syncing for Everyone! 🎉

We're thrilled to announce the release of Tidewell 2.4, our most powerful update yet. This release isn't just about new features — it's about reimagining how your team stays in sync.

## What's New

- **Offline Mode:** Keep working without a connection. Changes sync automatically when you're back online.
- **Faster Uploads:** Files up to 2 GB now upload up to 3x faster thanks to parallel chunking.
- **Audit Log Export:** Admins can export 90 days of audit history as CSV.

To upgrade, run `tidewell update --channel stable` or download the installer from our website.

Version 2.4 also drops support for macOS 11. As always, we're incredibly grateful to our community for their invaluable feedback. We can't wait to see what you build!
