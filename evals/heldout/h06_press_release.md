Northfield Community College Launches Groundbreaking Free Tuition Program for Local Students

NORTHFIELD, Minn. — Northfield Community College today announced the Northfield Promise, a transformative initiative that will cover full tuition for graduates of Rice County high schools beginning in fall 2027. The program, funded by a $6.2 million gift from the Hallvard Family Foundation, is expected to serve approximately 300 students each year.

"Every student in our county deserves a path to higher education, regardless of their family's income," said College President Dr. Renata Oyelaran. "The Northfield Promise removes the single biggest barrier our students face."

Students must enroll full-time and maintain a 2.0 GPA to remain eligible. The scholarship covers tuition and required fees but not books or housing.

"We believe that investing in young people is the best investment a community can make," said foundation chair Erik Hallvard.

This landmark program underscores Northfield Community College's unwavering commitment to access, equity, and excellence.
