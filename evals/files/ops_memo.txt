Subject: Exciting Changes to Our Shift Scheduling Process!

Hi Team,

I'm excited to announce that starting Monday, October 14, we'll be rolling out ShiftPilot, our new scheduling platform, across all three distribution centers (Reno, Sparks, and Fernley). This isn't just a new tool — it's a fundamental shift in how we approach work-life balance.

With ShiftPilot, you'll be able to swap shifts directly with coworkers, request time off from your phone, and see your schedule up to four weeks in advance. Managers will approve swaps within 24 hours. This will empower you to take control of your schedule, foster better communication, and enhance overall team morale.

Training sessions will be held on October 8 and October 10 at 2 p.m. in the Reno break room, and a recorded version will be available on the intranet for those who can't attend. The old paper sign-up sheets will be retired on October 31.

We understand that change can be challenging, but we're confident that ShiftPilot will make a real difference. Your feedback is invaluable as we navigate this journey together.

Let me know if you have any questions!

Best,
Marcus Oyelaran
Director of Operations
