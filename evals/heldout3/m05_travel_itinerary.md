# Three Perfect Days in Ljubljana

Nestled between the Alps and the Adriatic, Ljubljana is a hidden gem that effortlessly blends old-world charm with a vibrant, youthful energy. With its car-free center, dragon-guarded bridges, and riverside cafés, Slovenia's capital is the perfect destination for travelers seeking an unforgettable European getaway. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or an outdoor enthusiast, this enchanting city has something for everyone.

## Day 1: Old Town and the Castle

Start your morning at Prešeren Square, then cross the Triple Bridge into the Old Town. Ride the funicular up to Ljubljana Castle (€16 for the funicular and castle entry) and take in sweeping views of the red rooftops. In the evening, enjoy dinner along the Ljubljanica River.

## Day 2: Lake Bled Day Trip

Catch the 8:00 a.m. bus from the main station; the ride takes about 1 hour 20 minutes. Walk the 6-kilometer loop around the lake, row a pletna boat to the island church, and don't leave without trying the famous cream cake.

## Day 3: Markets and Metelkova

Browse the Central Market (closed Sundays) for local cheeses and honey, then spend the afternoon exploring Metelkova, the city's colorful alternative arts district.

Ljubljana may be small, but it truly leaves a big impression. Pack your bags and get ready to fall in love with this magical city!
