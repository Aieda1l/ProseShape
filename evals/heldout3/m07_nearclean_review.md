Bought this kettle in March after our old one started leaking from the base. It's been six months, so here's where I've landed.

The good: it boils a full liter in a little under four minutes, the temperature presets actually match what my thermometer says (I checked 80, 90 and 100), and the keep-warm setting holds for 30 minutes before shutting off, which is longer than I expected. The handle stays cool. The lid opens with one thumb, which matters more than you'd think at 6 a.m.

The less good: the water window is on the back, facing the wall, so I have to turn the whole thing around to see how much is in it. And the base beeps three times every time you lift it off. You can turn the beep off, but it took me a week to find the setting (hold the minus button for five seconds, if anyone else is wondering).

Would I buy it again? Yes. It's not the prettiest kettle on the counter, but it does the one job I need it to do, and it does it every morning without fuss.
