Subject: Important Update to Our Remote Work Policy

Hi everyone,

I hope this message finds you well! As part of our ongoing commitment to fostering a flexible, supportive, and productive work environment, we are excited to share some important updates to our remote work policy, effective July 1.

**What's changing:**

- **Core collaboration hours:** All employees will be expected to be available between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m. in their local time zone, Monday through Thursday.
- **In-office days:** Teams based within 50 miles of an office will work on-site two days per week. Each team lead will choose the days by June 13.
- **Home office stipend:** The annual stipend will increase from $400 to $650, reimbursable through Expensify.

**What's staying the same:**

- Fridays remain meeting-free.
- Employees hired as fully remote before January 1 of this year keep their current arrangement.

We understand that change can be challenging, and we want to emphasize that these updates reflect our deep commitment to both individual well-being and team connection. Your feedback has been invaluable in shaping this policy.

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR business partner or join our Q&A session on June 5 at 2 p.m. ET.

Thank you for all that you do!

Warm regards,
Danielle Fortier
VP of People Operations
