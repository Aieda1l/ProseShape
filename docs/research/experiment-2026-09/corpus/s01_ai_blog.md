In today's fast-paced urban landscape, getting around the city has never been more important. That's why Port Alder is thrilled to unveil PedalPoint, a groundbreaking bike-share program that's set to transform the way residents navigate their neighborhoods.

Launching on April 12, PedalPoint will deploy 1,200 bikes — including 400 e-bikes — across 85 stations, from the Old Mill District to the waterfront. But this isn't just about bikes; it's about building a more connected, sustainable, and vibrant community.

Riders can unlock a bike for $2, then pay 15 cents per minute. Annual members pay $90 and get the first 45 minutes of every ride free. The program is funded by a $3.4 million federal grant and a partnership with Alder County Transit.

At its core, PedalPoint represents a pivotal step toward a greener future. Whether you're commuting to work, running errands, or simply exploring the city, PedalPoint empowers you to move freely and confidently. The future of mobility is here — and it's on two wheels.
